Core features

Three things TabStack does

A focused toolkit to keep your browser fast, organized, and light.

Auto-Clustering

Stacks same-domain tabs into native groups.

Tabs from the same website cluster together automatically without cluttering your tab strip.

Active Focus

Expands your active site and folds the rest.

Switching tabs opens the current group and collapses inactive stacks out of your way.

RAM Hibernation

Frees background memory without losing state.

Puts dormant tabs to sleep at the browser level. Your scroll position and history stay put.

Zero configuration

How it works

Runs natively in Chromium with zero background overhead.

  1. 01

    Open your tabs

    Browse normally across YouTube, GitHub, docs, and search without worrying about clutter.

  2. 02

    Auto-group by site

    Same-domain tabs stack into clean, color-coded native groups in real time.

  3. 03

    Sleep idle stacks

    Background groups collapse and sleep automatically, reclaiming memory used by idle pages.

Pure privacy

What TabStack refuses to do

Built strictly on native Chromium extension APIs. No bloat, no tracking.

TabStack never... Why it matters
Injects scripts into your pages Your web pages remain 100% unmodified, fast, and secure.
Stores data remotely All preferences and saved sessions stay on your local device.
Modifies URLs No intermediate redirect pages, broken history, or lost bookmarks.
Phones home Zero analytics, zero telemetry, and 100% offline operation.
Requires an account Install it, open your tabs, and get straight to work.

Quick setup

Install TabStack

Runs on Chrome, Brave, Arc, Edge, and all Chromium browsers.

AltShiftT Stack now
AltShiftS Sleep dormant stacks
AltShiftA Toggle accordion focus

Developer Mode (Unpacked)

Zero-build install directly from the repository source.

  1. Clone or download this repository to your computer.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions) in your browser.
  3. Enable Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the tabstack folder.
