The tab bar problem

Three things it fixes

Less tab hunting. Less idle memory. More context when you switch tasks.

Focus

Your tab bar is unreadable past eight tabs.

TabStack collapses everything except the domain you are in. One group. One focus.

Memory

Idle tabs keep taking up space in RAM.

Dormant tabs are discarded by the browser when they sleep. Your place and history stay put.

Context

Every task switch makes you rebuild your workspace.

Switch to Pinterest and your YouTube and Spotify groups fold away automatically.

No setup ritual

How it works

TabStack stays out of your way and uses the browser features you already have.

  1. 01

    Browse normally

    Open tabs the way you always do. TabStack starts organizing in the background.

  2. 02

    Group by domain

    Tabs from the same site become one native browser group with no page changes.

  3. 03

    Sleep what you leave

    Inactive groups collapse and background tabs can be discarded to reclaim memory.

A small extension with clear boundaries

What TabStack doesn't do

The fastest way to understand a tool is to know what it refuses to touch.

TabStack never... Why it matters
Injects scripts into your pages Your pages stay exactly as the server sent them.
Stores data remotely Preferences and saved sessions stay on your machine.
Modifies URLs Your history, bookmarks, and scroll position remain intact.
Phones home Zero analytics, zero telemetry, and no remote server.
Requires an account Install it, open your tabs, and get back to work.

Try it without changing your workflow

Install TabStack

TabStack runs on Chrome, Brave, Arc, and Edge. There is no build step and no account to create.

Developer Mode (Unpacked)

Zero-build install directly from the repository source.

  1. Clone or download this repository to your computer.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions) in your browser.
  3. Enable Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the tabstack folder.
