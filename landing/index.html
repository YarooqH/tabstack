Pure privacy

What TabStack refuses to do

Built strictly on native Chromium extension APIs. No bloat, no tracking.

TabStack never... Why it matters
Injects scripts into your pages Your web pages remain 100% unmodified, fast, and secure.
Stores data remotely All preferences and saved sessions stay on your local device.
Modifies URLs No intermediate redirect pages, broken history, or lost bookmarks.
Phones home Zero analytics, zero telemetry, and 100% offline operation.
Requires an account Install it, open your tabs, and get straight to work.

Quick setup

Install TabStack

Runs on Chrome, Brave, Arc, Edge, and all Chromium browsers.

AltShiftT Stack now
AltShiftS Sleep dormant stacks
AltShiftA Toggle accordion focus

Developer Mode (Unpacked)

Zero-build install directly from the repository source.

  1. Clone or download this repository to your computer.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or brave://extensions) in your browser.
  3. Enable Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the tabstack folder.
